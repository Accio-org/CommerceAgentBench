# Freight Rate Schedule — Medical Equipment Division

Last updated: 2025-01-15

All rates are per-kilogram charges applicable to medical imaging equipment (HS Chapter 9022).
Rates are annual contract rates negotiated with our logistics partners.

## Notes

- Shanghai -> Delhi rate increase effective 2025-03-01 due to new fuel surcharge agreement with carrier (IndiaAir Cargo).
- All other major routes remain at 2024 contracted rates.
- Rates apply to standard medical equipment. Hazardous/oversized surcharges apply separately.
- Freight cost calculation: Total Weight (kg) x Applicable Rate (USD/kg) = Freight Cost (USD)
- 账务与重量口径按清单/CSV 的 supplier 字段完全一致匹配统计；名称变体（如缩写、后缀差异）不并入，另案处理。
