# Hidden Expected Facts for LLM Judge

- The product is HPT-750-SS Base model with LoopCap v2. The Pro titanium model is out of scope for this listing.
- Broad unqualified environmental copy such as "eco-friendly" and "better for the planet" should not survive in revised listing copy. FTC Green Guides §260.4 starts on PDF page 6 and Example 1 on page 7 addresses "Eco-friendly" as a likely broad general environmental benefit claim.
- LFGB food-contact and BPA-not-detected support are limited to the components and SKUs described in the certificate summaries.
- Cold 24h is supported for HPT-750-SS. Hot 24h is not supported; hot 12h is supported under the lab protocol.
- Leak testing supports narrowed leak-resistant wording under tested conditions, not absolute wording.
- The whole bottle is not dishwasher safe; only LoopCap v2 is top-rack dishwasher safe.
- The 30% recycled-steel statement needs more evidence and should not appear as a final listing claim. FTC Green Guides §260.13 starts on PDF page 27 and pages 27-29 cover recycled-content claims, percentages, and component qualification.
- Broad "non-toxic" and "safe for kids" copy should be narrowed or removed unless supported by competent evidence for the product and environment. FTC Green Guides §260.10 starts on PDF page 21.
- For broad safety wording, either removing the unsupported broad safety claim or revising it to component-specific LFGB/BPA facts can be acceptable if the final listing does not preserve "non-toxic" or "safe for kids" wording.
- FDA approved, customer-review-star, free delivery, discount, and time-sensitive promotional claims should be removed from product detail copy. Amazon Product Detail Page Rules page 2 prohibits price, delivery offers, reviews/testimonials, promotional material, and time-sensitive information.
- For "FDA approved", either a remove decision or a needs-more-evidence decision can be acceptable if the final listing excludes FDA approval wording and clearly uses only supported LFGB language.
- "Best Seller" should be removed from the title. Amazon Product Detail Page Style Guide page 4 says not to use subjective commentary such as "Hot Item" or "Best Seller."
- Pro titanium same-listing copy should be removed. Amazon Product Detail Page Rules page 3 says new versions with changes in color, size, material, features, or product name should be new detail pages; the Alibaba snapshot's Choose your model table separates Base Model, Advanced Model, and Pro Model.
- Good revised copy preserves commercial value by using supported claims: 750ml capacity, 304 stainless steel, LFGB food-contact materials, BPA-not-detected lid components, cold 24h, hot 12h, leak-resistant LoopCap v2, top-rack dishwasher-safe lid, hand-wash body.
