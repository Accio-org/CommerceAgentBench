# Container-Level Master Insurance Notes

This is **not** a separate policy in its own right. It is a
container-level co-loading memorandum that the consolidator (our
freight forwarder, MSC Logistics China) prepared when the five
customers' cargo was consolidated into container `OCEAN-2026-04-V552`
at Shanghai.

## Master cap (cargo-level only, across all five customers)

For internal claims-coordination purposes the consolidator has set a
**container-level aggregate cap of USD 130,000** on total insurance
payouts arising from a single container event. This is not a contract
between the insurers and the cargo owners; it is the consolidator's
internal threshold for triggering an escalation to their claims
manager and to the carrier (Hapag-Lloyd) for a potential carrier-level
recovery action.

If the sum of all five customers' settled claims exceeds USD 130,000
under a single container event, the consolidator will open a
container-level recovery file and pursue claims against the carrier
for the excess. Below USD 130,000, individual settlements are handled
customer-by-customer with no consolidator-level action.
