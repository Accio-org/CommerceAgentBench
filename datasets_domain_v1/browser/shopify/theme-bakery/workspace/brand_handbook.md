## 业务描述
Traditional French artisan bakery in the heart of the city. We use century-old sourdough starters, stone-ground organic flour, and 48-hour cold fermentation for every loaf. Our online store offers pre-orders for fresh-baked bread, pastries, and seasonal specials with same-day local delivery.

## 富文本
La Mie Dorée traces its roots to a small boulangerie in Lyon, France. Our master baker, trained under Meilleur Ouvrier de France laureates, brought those traditions to create something special: bread that tastes like it was meant to be. Every loaf begins with our 12-year-old sourdough starter, rested for 48 hours, and baked in our custom stone deck oven.
