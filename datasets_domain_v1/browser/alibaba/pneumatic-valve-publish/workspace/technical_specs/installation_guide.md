# Installation Guide — ZD-PBV Series Pneumatic Ball Valves

## Pre-Installation

1. Verify the valve model, size, and pressure rating match the piping system specifications.
2. Remove protective covers from flanges before installation.
3. Ensure the pipeline is clean and free of debris.
4. Check air supply pressure is within 4-7 bar range.

## Installation Steps

1. Position the valve between flanges, ensuring proper gasket alignment.
2. Insert bolts and tighten in a cross pattern to ensure even sealing.
3. Connect air supply lines to actuator ports (Port A: open, Port B: close).
4. Connect solenoid valve if applicable (refer to solenoid wiring diagram).
5. Perform a manual cycle test before pressurizing the system.

## Maintenance

- Inspect valve seats and seals every 12 months or 50,000 cycles.
- Lubricate actuator stem packing annually.
- Replace PTFE seats when leakage exceeds Class IV.
- Actuator spring return models: check spring tension every 24 months.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Possible Cause | Solution |
|-------|---------------|----------|
| Valve does not open | Low air pressure | Check supply >4 bar |
| Leakage through body | Damaged seat | Replace PTFE seat |
| Slow actuation | Undersized tubing | Use min. 8mm OD tubing |
| Actuator stuck | Corrosion buildup | Disassemble and clean |
