# Q3 2026 Pricing Memo — AccessoryHub Wholesale Catalogue

**Issued by:** Finance / Procurement  
**Effective:** 2026-07-01  
**Applies to:** AccessoryHub Wholesale Price List — Q3 2026

## Adjustment Rule

A **12% supplier surcharge** is applied to all **imported** products in the wholesale price list, effective Q3 2026, to reflect updated landed-cost estimates from overseas suppliers.

**Domestic** products are not affected and must remain at their current unit prices.

Items **re-sourced to a domestic supplier this quarter** are priced as domestic — no surcharge applies. Where the price list document includes a note indicating a product was re-sourced, treat that item as domestic for the purpose of this adjustment.

## Rounding

All adjusted prices must be rounded to the **nearest $0.05** (five-cent increment).

Examples of correct rounding:
- $15.37 → $15.35
- $22.63 → $22.65
- $9.724 → $9.70
- $41.48 → $41.50

## Exceptions

The following SKU is flagged as **discontinued** and must not be modified under any circumstances:

- **AG-190** (Webcam Privacy Cover, 10-pack)

## How to determine product origin

Each product line in the price list document states the product's origin (Imported or Domestic). Use that information to classify each item before applying the adjustment. Where a product line includes a re-sourcing note, that note takes precedence over the origin label.

## Summary of changes

| Origin / Status         | Action                                              |
|-------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Imported                | Multiply unit price × 1.12, round to nearest $0.05 |
| Domestic                | No change                                           |
| Re-sourced to domestic  | No change (treat as domestic; note in price list)   |
| Discontinued            | No change (regardless of origin)                    |
